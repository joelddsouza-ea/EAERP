# Express Autocare ERP — Quick Guide

One page per part of the system, in plain words. Each page says **what it is for**, **who uses it**,
**what to do every day**, and **how it connects** to the rest.

> Tip: if a menu item is missing, either your role does not use it, or the owner has switched that module
> off (Settings › Modules). Ask your manager.

---

## 0. Getting started (everyone)

**Sign in**
- Open the app link on your phone. Sign in with your **mobile number** (or email) and password.
- Install it like an app: browser menu › *Add to Home screen*.
- Forgot your password? Ask the owner/manager to reset it (Settings › Users).

**Find your way**
- **Menu** (left on a computer, *More* at the bottom right on a phone) is grouped: Home, CRM, Workshop,
  Customers, Parts & Stock, Billing, Accounts, People, Settings.
- The **bottom bar** on the phone shows the 4 screens you use most.
- **My Day** (Home) lists what you should do now — calls, messages, cars arriving, jobs, approvals. Start
  your day here.
- **Search** (magnifier at the top, or *Ctrl + K* on a computer): type a phone number, car number, job / bill /
  booking number or a name and jump straight to it.
- **Bell** at the top: alerts meant for you (new lead, job assigned, parts arrived, leave answered…). Tap to open.
- **Branch** selector (top, if you work at more than one branch) chooses whose data you see.

## 1. Attendance & leave (everyone) — *People › My Attendance*

**Punch in / out**
1. Be **at the workshop** with location (GPS) turned on.
2. Open **My Attendance** › tap **Punch in with a selfie** › the front camera opens › take the selfie.
3. Do the same when you leave (**Punch out**).
- The button only works inside the workshop area. A driver / home technician on a trip that day can
  punch from where they are.
- Forgot to punch? Tell your manager — they add it with a reason. You cannot edit punches yourself.

**Your month** — the calendar shows each day (green present, red absent, purple leave, grey weekly off).
Tap a day for times, late minutes and overtime.

**Leave** — *Leave* tab › **Apply for leave** › type, dates (or half day), reason › Send. Your manager gets a
bell and approves or rejects; you get a bell back. Paid leave beyond your balance is taken without pay.

**Payslips** — *Payslips* tab after the month's payroll is finalised; tap one for details or the PDF.

**Money** — *Advances* tab: salary advances still being recovered, and **customer money you hold** (see Billing).
Hand customer money over at the counter; anything still with you at month end comes off your salary.

**Connects to:** Payroll (days, overtime, leave), CRM (new leads skip people on leave).

## 2. CRM & leads (marketing executives, advisors) — *CRM*

**Where leads come from** — Meta / Google ads and the website arrive by themselves; incoming calls, walk-ins
and referrals you add with **Leads › Add lead** (mobile number is enough). A number that already has an open
lead is updated, not duplicated. Each new lead is given to an executive in turn; you get a bell.

**Every day — Leads › Today**
1. **Call now — new leads** (red): call within 15 minutes. Speed wins bookings. If you don't, you get a
   reminder, then your manager is told.
2. **Follow-ups due**: hot (red dot) first.

**On a lead**
- **Call** (green) → after the call, choose what happened. The next follow-up is set for you
  (no answer → try in 2 hours, then tomorrow, then in 3 days).
- **Send prices** → pick the plan (and car type) → the message with prices and a booking link is ready →
  **Send on WhatsApp**.
- **Book** → day, free time slot, plan or repair, drive-in / pickup / home (with address) → Book.
- **Close as lost** needs a reason. "Not now" reasons keep sending a gentle message every few weeks.
- **Stop messages** if the person asks.

**Messages to send** (*CRM › Messages to send*) — follow-up creatives, quotes and booking messages wait here.
Tap **Send on WhatsApp** → WhatsApp opens with the text → send → come back → the next one is ready.

**Bookings** (*CRM › Bookings*) — today's slots and who is coming.
- Online bookings (customers book themselves from their link) show **To confirm** — call and **Confirm**.
- **Car arrived** (drive-in) → check the car number / type → the **job card form opens already filled**.
- Pickup / home bookings open the job card and the driver's trip as soon as they are confirmed.
- **Move**, **Cancel**, or **Did not come** (also marked automatically 3 hours after the slot).

**What happens by itself** — after delivery the customer gets a thank-you with a 1-tap rating and a referral
link; low ratings alert the manager. Cars due for service, pending work, insurance renewal and customers who
have not come for months get reminders and appear as leads to call.

**Connects to:** Job cards (booking → job card), Billing (won leads carry the bill amount), Payroll
(incentive per converted lead), Customers (full history on the customer page).

## 3. Customers & vehicles (advisors, front desk) — *Customers*

- **Customers › New**: name, mobile, *How did they find us*. Then **Add Vehicle** (car number and **car type** —
  needed for plan prices).
- The customer page shows visits, total spent, rating, credit, next service due and one **history** of
  enquiries, bookings, jobs, bills and messages. **Book a service** from here.

**Connects to:** CRM, Job cards, Billing (customer credit).

---

## 4. Job cards & the workshop (advisors, technicians, managers) — *Workshop › Job Cards*

**Open a job card** — from a booking (*Car arrived*), or **Job Cards › New**: customer, car, service plan (or a
repair type), engine oil, odometer and fuel, customer complaints one per line. Then assign the technician(s) and bay.

**The job moves on by itself** through its stages: intake → estimate & approval → work → parts → trial → wash →
final QC → bill → gate pass → delivered. Each stage sends the customer its WhatsApp message (when set).

**Advisor**
- **Intake**: trial drive, photos / videos of the car.
- **Estimate**: plan lines plus additional work and parts → send to the customer; they approve or decline
  each line on their link (declined items are kept as "Recommended but not approved").
- Parts the technician needs: review and forward them to the parts coordinator.
- After the work: record **Trial drive OK** and **Washing done**.

**Technician** (*My Jobs*) — open the job sheet, mark each activity Done / N/A with the photos or videos it
asks for, add **Additional Issues** with a photo of the faulty part, list parts needed, **Submit to SA for Parts**.
Fit the new part only after it arrives (photo of the fitted part), then Done. Submit the sheet when finished.

**Manager** — final QC spot-check (photo within 30 seconds), override when needed (always with a reason).

**Connects to:** Parts, Billing (the bill is made from the estimate), CRM (delivery wins the lead, starts feedback).

## 5. Pickup, drop & home service (drivers, home technicians) — *My Trips* / *Pickups & Drops*

- **Driver**: *My Trips* lists your pickups and drops. **Start** → navigate → **Arrived** → odometer, fuel and
  photos/videos (the pickup *is* the intake) → **Collected**. For a drop you need the gate pass and the
  handover photos.
- **Home technician**: the visit has its own job sheet; close the visit with the photos asked for.
- **Advisor** books trips on the job card or from a CRM booking; the board shows today's trips.

**Connects to:** Job cards, Billing (pickup / home charges go on the bill), Attendance (punch from the trip).

---

## 6. Parts & purchases (parts coordinator) — *Parts & Stock*

- **Parts Desk**: parts requested by advisors → get supplier prices → after the customer approves, order them →
  mark them received (or receive them on a GRN, below). Technician and advisor get a bell.
- *If Purchases & stock is on:* **GRN** for every supplier bill — photo of each part, GST bill or not, credit or
  cash. The technician confirms right / wrong part. **Stock** for oil and consumables; **plan kits** are issued to
  jobs. **Suppliers** shows what we owe and records payments.

**Connects to:** Job cards, Billing (bill waits for parts on a GRN), Accounts (purchases and supplier dues).

---

## 7. Billing & payments (back office, advisors) — *Billing* and the job card's *Billing & Delivery*

1. Take **advances** any time (job card › Billing › Record payment). A payment can be split: cash + UPI + card.
   **Collected by staff**: the customer paid an employee's own UPI / cash — choose the employee; they hold it
   until handed over.
2. After final QC: **Make bill** (proforma) → check lines → **Approve** → the customer pays the balance.
3. **Convert to tax invoice** (or close a paid proforma).
4. Paid in full → the **gate pass** is issued by itself → **Vehicle out** delivers the job.
- Paid too much? **Refund** it or keep it as **customer credit** (usable on a later job).
- A gate pass before full payment needs the owner / manager and a reason.

**Connects to:** Accounts (every bill and payment is posted), CRM (bill amount = lead revenue), Attendance &
payroll (staff-held money).

---

## 8. Accounts (back office, owner) — *Accounts*

- **Nothing to type for bills, receipts, GRNs or payroll** — they post to the books by themselves.
- **Expenses**: *Add expense* — date, what for, paid to, amount, paid from (cash / bank / a supplier on credit /
  customer money held by staff), GST bill or not, photo of the bill.
- **Cash ↔ bank** (cash deposited) and **Journal voucher** for anything else; **Opening balances** once at the start.
- **Accounts & Reports**: Profit & loss, Balance sheet, Cash & bank book, Who owes (customers, suppliers, staff),
  **GST** summary for the CA, Day book, Ledgers, Trial balance, **Export CSV**.
- The owner can **lock** a finished month (*Settings › Chart of Accounts*).

**Connects to:** Billing, Parts, Payroll, CRM (referral rewards).

---

## 9. Payroll & staff (back office, owner, managers) — *People*

- **Attendance Register**: everyone's day with selfies; tap a person to add a missed punch or remove a wrong one
  (always with a reason). Month grid for the whole team.
- **Leave Requests**: approve / reject.
- **Employees**: designation, branch, shift, weekly off, joining date; salary, OT rate, off-day pay, PT / PF / ESI,
  bank (only those who prepare payroll see pay).
- **Payroll** each month: *Work out a month* → check each payslip (correct a line with a reason if needed) →
  the **owner finalises** after the month ends → **Mark paid**. Add incentives, bonus, deductions or penalties
  under *Manual lines*; automatic incentives come from the rules.
- **Staff Money & Advances**: give an advance (recovered monthly), record customer money handed over.

**Connects to:** Attendance, Billing (staff-held money), CRM (lead incentives), Accounts (salary cost and payments).

---

## 10. Settings (owner, managers) — *Settings*

All masters on one page, grouped and searchable: job sheets & stages, plans & prices, CRM (journeys —
which creative goes when —, creatives, lead sources & webhook links, campaigns & ad spend, booking slots,
lost reasons, rules), purchases, HR & payroll (pay & OT, shifts, holidays, leave types, incentive rules),
accounts, users, branches (workshop location for attendance), roles.

**Modules (owner)** — switch **Purchases & stock**, **Accounts**, **CRM & leads** and **Attendance & payroll**
on or off. Nothing is deleted. After switching Accounts back on, use *Bring the books up to date*.

## How it all connects — one car's journey

1. **Ad / call / referral** → **Lead** (CRM) → called, prices sent → **Booking**.
2. **Booking** → car arrives (or driver picks it up) → **Job card** filled in from the booking.
3. **Estimate** → customer approves on WhatsApp → **Parts** ordered / received (GRN) → technician works.
4. Trial, wash, final QC → **Bill** → **Payments** (maybe collected by staff) → **Gate pass** → **Vehicle out**.
5. **Accounts** record the sale, GST, payments and parts cost by themselves.
6. **CRM** marks the lead won with the bill amount → thank-you + rating + referral link → next service reminder.
7. **Payroll** counts attendance, overtime and incentives (including converted leads) at month end.
