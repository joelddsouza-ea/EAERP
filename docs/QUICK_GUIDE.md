# Express Autocare ERP — Quick Guide

One page per part of the system, in plain words. Each page says **what it is for**, **who uses it**,
**what to do every day**, and **how it connects** to the rest.

> Tip: if a menu item is missing, either your role does not use it, or the owner has switched that module
> off (Settings › Modules). Ask your manager.

---

## 0. Getting started (everyone)

**Sign in**
- Open the app link on your phone. Sign in with your **mobile number** (or email) and password.
- Install it like an app: browser menu › *Add to Home screen*.
- Forgot your password? Ask the owner/manager to reset it (Settings › Users).

**Find your way**
- **Menu** (left on a computer, *More* at the bottom right on a phone) is grouped: Home, CRM, Workshop,
  Customers, Parts & Stock, Billing, Accounts, People, Settings.
- The **bottom bar** on the phone shows the 4 screens you use most.
- **My Day** (Home) lists what you should do now — calls, messages, cars arriving, jobs, approvals. Start
  your day here.
- **Search** (magnifier at the top, or *Ctrl + K* on a computer): type a phone number, car number, job / bill /
  booking number or a name and jump straight to it.
- **Bell** at the top: alerts meant for you (new lead, job assigned, parts arrived, leave answered…). Tap to open.
- **Branch** selector (top, if you work at more than one branch) chooses whose data you see.

## 1. Attendance & leave (everyone) — *People › My Attendance*

**Punch in / out**
1. Be **at the workshop** with location (GPS) turned on.
2. Open **My Attendance** › tap **Punch in with a selfie** › the front camera opens › take the selfie.
3. Do the same when you leave (**Punch out**).
- The button only works inside the workshop area. A driver / home technician on a trip that day can
  punch from where they are.
- Forgot to punch? Tell your manager — they add it with a reason. You cannot edit punches yourself.

**Location sharing while punched in** — from punch in to punch out the app shares your location with the
owner and managers (the first time, it shows what is shared and asks you to confirm). A small green line at
the top of every screen says *Sharing your location until you punch out*; a red one means location is off or
blocked — turn it on. In the browser it works only while the app is open; **field staff should install the
Android app** (it keeps sharing with the screen locked and shows a notification). Nothing is shared after
punch out or on days off.

**Your month** — the calendar shows each day (green present, red absent, purple leave, grey weekly off).
Tap a day for times, late minutes and overtime.

**Leave** — *Leave* tab › **Apply for leave** › type, dates (or half day), reason › Send. Your manager gets a
bell and approves or rejects; you get a bell back. Paid leave beyond your balance is taken without pay.

**Payslips** — *Payslips* tab after the month's payroll is finalised; tap one for details or the PDF.

**Money** — *Advances* tab: salary advances still being recovered, and **customer money you hold** (see Billing).
Hand customer money over at the counter; anything still with you at month end comes off your salary.

**Connects to:** Payroll (days, overtime, leave), CRM (new leads skip people on leave).

## 2. CRM & leads (marketing executives, advisors) — *CRM*

**Where leads come from** — Meta / Google ads and the website arrive by themselves; incoming calls, walk-ins
and referrals you add with **Leads › Add lead** (mobile number is enough). A number that already has an open
lead is updated, not duplicated. Each new lead is given to an executive in turn; you get a bell.

**Every day — Leads › Today**
1. **Call now — new leads** (red): call within 15 minutes. Speed wins bookings. If you don't, you get a
   reminder, then your manager is told.
2. **Follow-ups due**: hot (red dot) first.

**On a lead**
- **Call** (green) → after the call, choose what happened. The next follow-up is set for you
  (no answer → try in 2 hours, then tomorrow, then in 3 days).
- **Send prices** → pick the plan (and car type) → the message with prices and a booking link is ready →
  **Send on WhatsApp**.
- **Book** → day, free time slot, plan or repair, drive-in / pickup / home (with address) → Book.
- **Close as lost** needs a reason. "Not now" reasons keep sending a gentle message every few weeks.
- **Stop messages** if the person asks.

**Messages to send** (*CRM › Messages to send*) — follow-up creatives, quotes and booking messages wait here.
Tap **Send on WhatsApp** → WhatsApp opens with the text → send → come back → the next one is ready.

**WhatsApp Inbox** (*CRM › WhatsApp Inbox*, when Interakt is on) — customers' and leads' WhatsApp chats.
- **Mine** = your leads' chats, your open jobs' customers, and chats given to you; **Nobody's** = not yet taken
  (open one and *Give to…* yourself). Managers also see **All**. A green number = unread; you also get a bell.
- **Someone new writes in → a lead is made by itself** (source *WhatsApp chat*), given to an executive in turn.
  If their first message is a campaign's *WhatsApp start text* (Click-to-WhatsApp ads), the lead is that
  campaign's. A customer with a car in the workshop goes to that job's advisor instead (no lead).
- **Reply** with text, a photo or a PDF for **24 hours after their last message** (WhatsApp's rule; the box
  shows the time left). After that only an approved template can go (e.g. *Chat opener*); their reply opens the
  chat again.
- Ticks: ✓ sent, ✓✓ delivered, blue ✓✓ read, ⚠ not delivered (the reason shows). Job and booking messages sent
  by the system appear in the same chat. **Make lead**, **Close** / **Reopen** from the top of the chat.
- Button taps on our messages (e.g. *Interested*, *Call me*, *Not now*) show in the chat and move the lead by
  themselves (interested / call now with a bell / follow up in 30 days).
- The lead page shows the same chat. A reply of **STOP** ends marketing messages to that number.

**Bookings** (*CRM › Bookings*) — today's slots and who is coming.
- Online bookings (customers book themselves from their link) show **To confirm** — call and **Confirm**.
- **Car arrived** (drive-in) → check the car number / type → the **job card form opens already filled**.
- Pickup / home bookings open the job card and the driver's trip as soon as they are confirmed.
- **Move**, **Cancel**, or **Did not come** (also marked automatically 3 hours after the slot).

**What happens by itself** — after delivery the customer gets a thank-you with a 1-tap rating and a referral
link; low ratings alert the manager. Cars due for service, pending work, insurance renewal and customers who
have not come for months get reminders and appear as leads to call.

**Connects to:** Job cards (booking → job card), Billing (won leads carry the bill amount), Payroll
(incentive per converted lead), Customers (full history on the customer page).

## 3. Customers & vehicles (advisors, front desk) — *Customers*

- **Customers › New**: name, mobile, *How did they find us*. Then **Add Vehicle** (car number and **car type** —
  needed for plan prices).
- The customer page shows visits, total spent, rating, credit, next service due and one **history** of
  enquiries, bookings, jobs, bills and messages. **Book a service** from here.

**Connects to:** CRM, Job cards, Billing (customer credit).

## 4. Job cards & the workshop (advisors, technicians, managers) — *Workshop › Job Cards*

**Open a job card** — from a booking (*Car arrived*), or **Job Cards › New**: customer, car, service plan (or a
repair type), engine oil, odometer and fuel, customer complaints one per line. Optional: tick **Wheel alignment &
balancing** and choose the outside partner (they are told and upload photos/videos), and untick **Show the progress
timeline** to hide the stage list from the customer (also on the job card's *Outside work* card). Then assign the
technician(s) and bay.

**The job moves on by itself** through its stages: intake → estimate & approval → work (the trial drive and the
wash are sections of the technician's job sheet) → parts → final QC → bill → gate pass → delivered. Each stage
sends the customer its WhatsApp message (when set).

**Advisor**
- **Intake**: trial drive, photos / videos of the car.
- **Estimate**: plan lines plus additional work and parts → send to the customer; they approve or decline
  each line on their link (declined items are kept as "Recommended but not approved").
- Parts the technician needs: review and forward them to the parts coordinator.
- **Updates to send** (bell): when a technician or driver finishes a step they tap *Send update*; the message
  is ready in your bell — read it and tap **Send on WhatsApp** (or *Skip*).
- After the technician submits: tap the red **Send to back office for bill** straight away (back office starts the bill
  while you do the **final QC** / pre-delivery checklist). The customer gets the bill only once the car is ready.
- **Pickup jobs**: the driver's photos and videos are the intake — don't take them again; do the **trial drive** at the
  workshop and record it to complete the intake. On a New Job Card for pickup & drop, choose the **driver** there.
- **Estimate**: each part / labour line can be priced **including GST** or **excluding GST** (choose the GST rate);
  excluded lines get GST added for the customer. After a call you can still change a customer's decision with
  **Record customer decision** up to the bill being approved: the coordinator is told (buy / cancel / return) and the
  customer gets a message showing what changed and the new total.
- **Mobile numbers** are 10 digits; car numbers are saved in capitals.

**Technician** (*My Jobs*) — open the job sheet, mark each activity Done / N/A with the photos or videos it
asks for, add **Additional Issues** with a photo of the faulty part, list parts needed, **Submit to SA for Parts**.
When a new part arrives, check it in person and tap **Right part** or **Wrong part** (the coordinator's GRN photo
is not shown). Fit it (photo of the fitted part), then Done.
- **Diesel / electric cars**: spark-plug points are N/A and can't be changed. Points marked **If applicable** (violet)
  are done only when they apply.
- **Additional Issues** has two parts: *Customer complaints & trial-drive observations* and *Found by the technician*.
  Remove your own line if it repeats a customer complaint. A service point flagged ⚠ and finished on its Additional
  Issues line shows as **Done via Additional Issues** — no more photos needed there.
- **Final Checks** (trial drive) come before **Washing & Cleaning**. After the trial drive tap **Send to washing**
  and choose the washing partner. Add the photos/videos the washer sends on WhatsApp from the gallery (or the
  washer uploads them from their own login), tap **Car is back**, finish the washing section and **submit** —
  the job goes to the advisor's final QC.
- After each section you can tap **Send update**: the advisor gets the WhatsApp ready to send to the customer.

**Outside partners** (washing, wheel alignment & balancing) sign in with their mobile number and see only the cars
sent to them. The washer fills the same washing points as the technician's sheet (Vacuum, Dashboard wipe / polish, Body
wash…) with the photos / videos the masters ask for (camera or gallery), then taps **Done**. Set them up in *Settings › Outside Service Partners* (several per
kind; *Create login*).

**Photos and videos**: tap **Take photo** or **Record video** — the camera opens straight away (one shot per tap;
tap again for more). **From gallery** appears only where it is allowed (washing, outside partners, customers).

**Manager** — final QC spot-check (photo within 30 seconds), override when needed (always with a reason).
**Technician Time** (*Workshop › Technician Time*, owner and managers): how long each technician worked on each job —
time in work, less waiting for approval or parts and time at outside partners, counted in shift hours only (no
weekly off, holidays, leave; break taken out). Every technician on a job gets its full time. CSV download; the
job card's Summary shows the same for that job.

**Connects to:** Parts, Billing (the bill is made from the estimate), CRM (delivery wins the lead, starts feedback).

## 5. Pickup, drop & home service (drivers, home technicians) — *My Trips* / *Pickups & Drops*

- **Driver**: *My Trips* lists your pickups and drops. **Start** → navigate → **Arrived** → odometer, fuel and
  photos/videos (the pickup *is* the intake) → **Collected**. For a drop you need the gate pass and the
  handover photos. After a step tap **Send update**: the advisor sends the customer the WhatsApp.
- **Home technician**: the visit has its own job sheet; close the visit with the photos asked for.
- **Advisor** books trips on the job card or from a CRM booking; the board shows today's trips.

**Connects to:** Job cards, Billing (pickup / home charges go on the bill), Attendance (punch from the trip).

## 6. Parts & purchases (parts coordinator) — *Parts & Stock*

- **Parts Desk**: parts requested by advisors → get supplier prices → after the customer approves, order them →
  mark them received (or receive them on a GRN, below). Technician and advisor get a bell.
- *If Purchases & stock is on:* **GRN** for every supplier bill — photo of each part, GST bill or not, credit or
  cash. The technician checks the part in person and confirms right / wrong part (the GRN photo is for the records). **Stock** for oil and consumables; **plan kits** are issued to
  jobs **by themselves when the technician submits** (final QC) — whatever is short in stock is shown on the job card
  and the coordinator gets a bell; issue it with *Issue item* once received. A part we keep in stock: on the Parts
  Desk choose **Take from stock…** instead of supplier prices — priced at the average cost (plus the usual margin on
  the estimate), issued to the job when the customer approves, billed without a GRN. **Suppliers** shows what we owe and records payments.

**Connects to:** Job cards, Billing (bill waits for parts on a GRN), Accounts (purchases and supplier dues).

## 7. Billing & payments (back office, advisors) — *Billing* and the job card's *Billing & Delivery*

1. Take **advances** any time (job card › Billing › Record payment). A payment can be split: cash + UPI + card.
   **Collected by staff**: the customer paid an employee's own UPI / cash — choose the employee; they hold it
   until handed over.
2. When the technician submits (final QC) the advisor taps the red **Send to back office for bill**. Back office gets a
   bell, a red banner on the job card and the cards *Bills to make* / *Bills to approve* (Dashboard and My Day). **Make proforma from estimate** → recheck each line (**edit**: quantity, cost, selling price, HSN; the
   margin is shown) → **Approve & send to customer** → send the ready bill message on WhatsApp → the customer pays.
   The advisor can do all of this too, and record payments.
3. **Convert to tax invoice** or close a paid proforma — back office, managers and the owner only (also refunds).
4. Paid in full → the **gate pass** is issued by itself → **Vehicle out** delivers the job.
- Paid too much? **Refund** it or keep it as **customer credit** (usable on a later job).
- A gate pass before full payment needs the owner / manager and a reason.

**Connects to:** Accounts (every bill and payment is posted), CRM (bill amount = lead revenue), Attendance &
payroll (staff-held money).

## 8. Accounts (back office, owner) — *Accounts*

- **Nothing to type for bills, receipts, GRNs or payroll** — they post to the books by themselves.
- **Expenses**: *Add expense* — date, what for, paid to, amount, paid from (cash / bank / a supplier on credit /
  customer money held by staff), GST bill or not, photo of the bill.
- **Cash ↔ bank** (cash deposited) and **Journal voucher** for anything else; **Opening balances** once at the start.
- **Accounts & Reports**: Profit & loss, Balance sheet, Cash & bank book, Who owes (customers, suppliers, staff),
  **GST** summary for the CA, Day book, Ledgers, Trial balance, **Export CSV**.
- The owner can **lock** a finished month (*Settings › Chart of Accounts*).

**Connects to:** Billing, Parts, Payroll, CRM (referral rewards).

## 9. Payroll & staff (back office, owner, managers) — *People*

- **Live Staff Map** (owner, managers): everyone punched in today on a map — moving, not moving, **no signal**
  (phone off / app closed / location blocked), battery, which pickup or home visit they're on. Tap a person for
  their route: km travelled, stops and gaps, with a time slider; pick a date for an earlier day.
  Switch it off for one employee in *Employees*, or for everyone in *Settings › Pay, OT, Statutory & Live Location*.
- **Attendance Register**: everyone's day with selfies; tap a person to add a missed punch or remove a wrong one
  (always with a reason). Month grid for the whole team.
- **Leave Requests**: approve / reject.
- **Employees**: designation, branch, shift, weekly off, joining date; salary, OT rate, off-day pay, PT / PF / ESI,
  bank (only those who prepare payroll see pay).
- **Payroll** each month: *Work out a month* → check each payslip (correct a line with a reason if needed) →
  the **owner finalises** after the month ends → **Mark paid**. Add incentives, bonus, deductions or penalties
  under *Manual lines*; automatic incentives come from the rules.
- **Staff Money & Advances**: give an advance (recovered monthly), record customer money handed over.

**Connects to:** Attendance, Billing (staff-held money), CRM (lead incentives), Accounts (salary cost and payments).

## 10. Settings (owner, managers) — *Settings*

**Roles & Access** — tick what each role may do (by module) and, on the *Dashboard* tab, which dashboard blocks it sees
(money figures for the owner, managers and back office only, by default). The owner role always has everything.
**Fleet**: on a fleet customer's page tick the **cabs the owner drives** himself (one login, no driver limit).
**Weekly off** (Branches) also closes that day in the booking calendar; the other days use Monday's booking slots.

All masters on one page, grouped and searchable: job sheets & stages, plans & prices, CRM (journeys —
which creative goes when —, creatives, lead sources & webhook links, campaigns & ad spend, booking slots,
lost reasons, rules), purchases, HR & payroll (pay & OT, shifts, holidays, leave types, incentive rules),
accounts, users, branches (workshop location for attendance), roles.

**Modules (owner)** — switch **Purchases & stock**, **Accounts**, **CRM & leads** and **Attendance & payroll**
on or off. Nothing is deleted. After switching Accounts back on, use *Bring the books up to date*.

**WhatsApp & Interakt (owner)** — *Settings › CRM › WhatsApp & Interakt* (was *Settings › WhatsApp*).
- *Connection*: *Share links* (default: staff tap to send from the workshop phone) or **Interakt** (WhatsApp
  Business API). Paste the API key; copy the **webhook URL** into Interakt › Developer Settings, and enter the
  same **webhook secret key** in both places. Switches: *new chats become leads*, *send contacts / events to
  Interakt*, *give chats to the same person in Interakt*. The card "What Interakt has sent us" shows if chats,
  ticks and taps are arriving — incoming chats need Interakt's higher plan.
- *Templates & buttons*: **Fetch from Interakt** lists your approved templates (with their status); for each
  message pick its template (or copy the "Text for Interakt", get it approved, then pick it). For quick-reply
  buttons choose what a tap does: lead interested / call me / not now. A rejected template gives the owner a bell.
- *Contacts & events*: leads and customers go to Interakt with their details and tags every 15 minutes; events
  (LeadCreated, BookingConfirmed, JobDelivered, ServiceDue …) can start an Interakt ongoing campaign.
  **Send all contacts now** for the first sync.
- Messages without an approved template stay in *Messages to send*. Switch back to share links any time.

**App Address & Customer App (owner)** — the public address of the system (every link sent to customers uses it;
leave empty while testing), with a QR code for setting up staff phones, the UPI ID customers pay to, and the
helpline number. **AI Assistant (owner)** — the customer app's assistant: Anthropic key, model, daily limit,
monthly budget, body-shop price guide, and what it was asked.

## 11. Customer app (advisors, back office) — what customers see at *<address>/app*

- Customers open any WhatsApp link from us (signed in at once) or sign in with their number and a code. **When a
  customer asks for a code, open their page (Customers) › *Customer app* card and read the code out** (only to
  their own number). You also get a bell.
- In the app they track the job live (driver on a map), approve or decline items with photos, pay by UPI, see
  their service book, book a service at their car's price, keep insurance / PUC / RC, and ask the assistant.
- **UPI payments**: *Billing › UPI Payments to Confirm* — check the UTR in the bank, then *Confirm & record*
  (the payment is recorded on the job and the customer told) or *Reject* with a reason they see.
- App bookings arrive in *CRM › Bookings* like web bookings ("Booked online"), with a bell: call to confirm.
  Accident-help requests arrive as body-shop leads.

## 12. Fleet cabs & drivers (advisors, managers, owner) — *Customers* and *Customers › Fleet Reports*

- **New fleet**: *Customers › Add Customer*, Customer Type **Fleet (cab owner)** with the owner's name and phone.
  Saving opens **Fleet setup**: add the cabs one after another (make and model stay filled in), then **Add driver**
  (name, mobile, the cabs they drive), then *Finish*. The fleet owner can also add drivers in the app.
- **Existing customer**: open the customer › *Fleet* card › tick **Cab fleet** (their type becomes Fleet).
- **Drivers sign in** to the customer app with their own mobile number (code read out from the fleet's page, or
  sent by WhatsApp), or with the link they get. They see only their cabs; no wallet, no adding cars.
- **Driver's approval limit**: a driver can approve extra work up to a total per job — the default is in
  *Masters › Fleet Cabs*; a fleet can have its own on its *Fleet* card. Anything
  above it waits for the fleet owner: they get an app note and a WhatsApp message in *Messages to send*, and the
  advisor gets a bell. A driver can decline; they can't change what the owner decided.
- **Job card**: for a fleet cab choose the **Driver who brought the cab**. That driver hears about stages in the
  app and gets a WhatsApp "cab ready" message with their own link. Fleets pay per job, as usual.
- **Fleet Reports**: problems drivers report (breakdowns first, with photos, km and location). **Book in**
  (drive-in or pickup from where the cab stands), **Open job card**, or **Close** with a note. A job card opened
  for the cab picks its open reports up by itself. The driver and the owner are told every step.
- **Km**: drivers log the odometer in the app; job cards add theirs. Cabs within the warning km of their next
  service get a reminder (owner and drivers) once.
- The owner's **Fleet dashboard** (in the app): every cab at the workshop or on the road, approvals waiting,
  money due, km and next service, spend this month / year per cab.
- Whole feature: *Settings › Modules › Fleet cabs & drivers*.
- Customer app: drivers and the fleet owner see the screens in *More* (Fleet dashboard, Drivers, Problem
  reports) and on a cab (*Report a problem*, *Log km*).

## How it all connects — one car's journey

1. **Ad / call / referral** → **Lead** (CRM) → called, prices sent → **Booking**.
2. **Booking** → car arrives (or driver picks it up) → **Job card** filled in from the booking.
3. **Estimate** → customer approves on WhatsApp → **Parts** ordered / received (GRN) → technician works.
4. Trial drive and wash (outside partner) on the job sheet → final QC → advisor sends for billing → **Bill** →
   **Payments** (maybe collected by staff) → **Gate pass** → **Vehicle out**.
5. **Accounts** record the sale, GST, payments and parts cost by themselves.
6. **CRM** marks the lead won with the bill amount → thank-you + rating + referral link → next service reminder.
7. **Payroll** counts attendance, overtime and incentives (including converted leads) at month end.
