## Overview
An ERP for Car Service Workshop ERP, handling different locations and different functions of business.
1. Accidental Repairs
2. Mechanical Repairs
3. Tailored Service Plans for different Classes of Cars
4. Home Service on Wheels
5. Detailing
6. AC repairs and service
7. Electrical 

## Purpose
1. Business owner should be able to see the entire business overview in single dashboard.
   which should contain.
   1. Jobs in progress.
   2. Daily Collection 
   3. Billing Till Date
   4. Total Outstanding.

## Users of System
1. Owner - to see overall status of the operations and financial 
2. Manager - OVersees All location business operations. Ensure each location  has enough business and meet business target for all locations.
3. Service Advisors - To manage Technicians, Spare part Procurement, Thrid Party Services, Ensure minimal TAT.
4. Technicians - To execute job assigned by service advisors.
5. Spare Part Coordinator.
6. Backoffice Excecutive - Billing/ Payments/ Accounts / Inventory mangement 
7. Marketing Executive - Convert Leads to Sales. Followup on existing customers
8. Body shop mangager : OVersees All location body shop operations. Ensure each location  has enough business and meet business target for all locations.
9. Body shop Advisors :  To manage Bodyshop Technicians, Spare part Procurement, Thrid Party Services, Ensure minimal TAT.
10. Body shop Technicians - To execute job assigned by Body shop advisors .
